Sector 01 · Mission plan

One night, three stages

You bring the crew and the guest of honour, and base takes care of the rest. Here’s how your tour runs:

Stage 01 · Cocktails in the Space Cantina

Dock and settle in: we kick things off with a toast in our Space Cantina, perhaps with a “Qui Gon Gin”. Time to raise the first glass to the one who’s about to swap surnames.

Stage 02 · A one-hour Padawan tour

Then you set off as Padawans through more than 1,200 m² of exhibition: the 5.80-metre AT-AT, Han Solo in carbonite, rarities and the stories behind the films. No prior knowledge needed. The jaw-dropping happens all by itself.

Stage 03 · Lightsaber combat training

The grand finale: lightsabers on, take your stance. Anyone who shows staying power here is ready for marriage. Photos are very much encouraged: you bring the poses, we provide the backdrop.
