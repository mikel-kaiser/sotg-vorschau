Sector 01 · Mission briefing

Four missions to earn a Jedi pass

Your party host leads the crew right through the sci-fi museum, from station to station. No child left twiddling their thumbs, no parent stuck explaining the games.

Mission 01 · Robot repair

Screwdrivers at the ready: the junior engineers team up to assemble little droids and get them back on course.

Mission 02 · Sound quiz: “Name that galaxy”

Listen up: which hum belongs to which starship, which beep to which droid? The sharpest ears win.

Mission 03 · Laser target game

Steady hand, sharp eye: taking aim soon shows who has what it takes to be a pilot.

Mission 04 · Lightsaber training

With glowing blades, the Padawans practise their stance and duelling. It’s the photo op of the day, without a single bruise.

Mission objective · The Jedi pass

Every child completes their training with their very own Jedi pass. Souvenir and knighthood in one.

Sector 06 · Questions

Parents’ questions for command centre

My child has an allergy. And can we bring our own cake?

We prefer to sort out both personally in advance. Drop us an email at info@starsofthegalaxy.de before you book, and we’ll work out together what’s possible for your party.

How many children and adults can come along?

Each party has a maximum of 10 children, joined by 2–3 accompanying adults. Enough pairs of eyes for photos. Our party host takes care of the rest.

Can we book a day other than Saturday?

The fixed start times are Saturdays at 12:30 and 15:00. On weekdays we celebrate by arrangement; on Sundays by arrangement and for a surcharge. Just enter your preferred date in the booking form and we’ll get back to you by email.

We’re running late or have a last-minute question. What now?

Send us an email at info@starsofthegalaxy.de. We’ll get back to you as quickly as we can. Remember: the booking itself is always made in writing, and you’ll receive a confirmation by email.

Is a booking via the form binding straight away?

Your booking becomes binding as soon as we confirm it by email. Before that, we check the date is free. To be safe, check your spam folder too. Just have a question for now? Then it’s better to use our contact form or give us a call: +49 2161 406 37 90.

What’s in the party bags?

For €5 per bag you get contents worth over €10: a LEGO-compatible figure and trading cards, temporary tattoos, mini figures to paint and sweets. Ideal as a take-home treat for your guests. Just choose how many in the booking form.

Our guests never know what to give. Any ideas?

Yes: gift vouchers from the guest children. We’ll give you gift vouchers worth €5 or €10 in advance, on a sale-or-return basis. On the day, you only pay for what was sold, and the rest comes back to us. Just note roughly how many you need in the form.
