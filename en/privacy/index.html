Privacy policy

This translation is provided for your convenience. The German version is legally binding: Datenschutzerklärung

The short version first: this website cares about the galaxy, not about your data. No tracking, no analytics tools, cookies only with your consent. Whatever does get processed is listed here. Short and sweet, with no small print hidden in the small print.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:

Thomas Manglitz

2. Hosting & server log files

This website is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. We have a data processing agreement with the host in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR.

Whenever pages are accessed, the server automatically stores what are known as log files. They contain:

  • the IP address of the requesting device,
  • the date and time of access,
  • the page requested,
  • the user agent (browser type and operating system).

We need this data to keep the site running stably and securely, for example to detect attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in technically error-free operation). The log files are deleted in line with the host’s retention periods. They are not combined with any other data.

3. Contact and enquiry forms

This website has several forms: for general contact enquiries and for enquiries about prop rental, hen & stag parties and kids’ birthday parties. Whatever you enter there (e.g. your name, email address, preferred date and message) is processed solely to handle and answer your enquiry. The details are sent by email to info@starsofthegalaxy.de.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract (e.g. booking a birthday party or a guided tour), and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). We do not pass your data on to third parties. We delete it once your enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

4. Google Maps: link only

We do not embed a map on the contact page; we link to Google Maps instead. Only when you click the link does Google Maps open in a new window. Only then is data (including your IP address) transferred to Google, and Google’s terms apply. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

For more details, see Google’s privacy policy.

5. Videos: only on click

The videos on this website (the WDR report and the guide to the AR app SnoopIT) are hosted on our own server and only play after you have started them with a click. No data is passed on to third parties. Only if a video file is ever unavailable is the youtube-nocookie.com player loaded instead, in privacy-enhanced mode and likewise only after your click.

In that case, when you start the video, data (including your IP address) is transferred to YouTube or Google. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. It applies only to the video you actually start. For more details, see Google’s privacy policy.

6. Cookies & consent

On your first visit, a pop-up asks which cookies you want to allow. Necessary entries are always active: they store your cookie choice (in browser storage under “sotg-cookie-consent-v1”, which stays on your device) and use sessionStorage to stop notices from popping up again every time you change page. These entries are not sent to anyone.

Cookies in the preferences, statistics and marketing categories are only set with your consent and only if a corresponding service is actually in use. There are currently no analytics or advertising tools active on this website; if you decline, everything simply stays off. Should services such as Google Tag Manager or the Meta Pixel be added in future, they will only load with your consent, and this section will be updated accordingly.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page.

Separately: if a video is loaded from YouTube as a fallback (see section 5), Google may set cookies within the embedded window. Your click counts as consent. Nothing happens before that.

7. External links & services

Some services are provided by external companies. As soon as you switch over to them, their privacy notices apply instead of this policy:

  • Ticket and merch shop (Ticket shop, Merch shop): run on the Shopify platform. See Shopify’s privacy policy.
  • Digital exhibition guide (in five languages, at /en/guide/): displayed as an Adobe Express embed directly on our site. The embed only loads when you click a language. Your click counts as consent, and from then on Adobe may set cookies within the embedded window (as with YouTube above). See Adobe’s privacy policy.
  • AR app “SnoopIT” (download link): a third-party service. The privacy notices of the app or of the respective app store apply.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights. An informal email to info@starsofthegalaxy.de is all it takes:

  • Access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • Withdrawal of consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR), for example for YouTube videos started with a click.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW) (the North Rhine-Westphalia Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.
