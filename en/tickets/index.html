Sector 04 · Comms channel

FAQs from orbit

Are you open on public holidays?

Yes. Our usual opening hours apply on public holidays too, for example on Good Friday or Ascension Day: Thursdays and Fridays from 12:00 to 18:00, Saturdays from 10:00 to 18:00 and Sundays from 12:00 to 17:00. We’re also open on Easter Monday and Whit Monday from 12:00 to 17:00. Over Christmas we take a short break; the exact dates are shown in the opening status at the top of the page.

How much are tickets?

Children aged 3 to 15 pay €12, adults aged 16 and over €18. The family ticket costs €50 and covers 2 adults + 2 children or 1 adult + 3 children. You don’t even have to be a family: the ticket works for any group of 2 adults and 2 children, whether you’re friends or neighbours. Concessions are €8 for children and €14 for adults. Online or at the ticket desk, the price is the same.

How long does a visit take?

There’s no standard orbit time: some visitors move through briskly, while fans stay a good deal longer, as there’s plenty to discover across 1,200 m². Best to allow a little extra time: the Hoth diorama doesn’t let anyone go in a hurry.

Where can I park?

There are free parking bays right outside the building, a larger pay-and-display car park opposite and a multi-storey car park about 100 m away. Bonus tip: the ticket desk will give you a discount coupon for the Lüpertzender Str. 86 car park (first hour free, then €2 per hour).

Is it true that the museum is cold?

Yes. Our museum is unheated. Dress as if you were off on an expedition to an ice planet, and you’ll easily last until the very last exhibit.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome here. In the interests of diplomatic relations, you should know that two cats live here too.

Can I take photos?

Yes, you’re very welcome to take photos. The 5.80-metre AT-AT and Han Solo in carbonite are already waiting for your best shot.

Can a kids’ birthday party be combined with a guided tour?

From the Medium birthday package upwards, a visit to the exhibition is already included. Regular guided tours take place on Saturdays and are booked by email. If you’d like to combine things, just write to us at info@starsofthegalaxy.de. We’ll get back to you with the options.
